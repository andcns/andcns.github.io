Devlog 03 / 2 November 2026 · Board v0.1

The final board pass focused on making the landscape feel like one miniature countryside rather than 64 decorated hexes.

Controlled natural variation

I expanded tree families, grass, crops, rocks and small scatter. Placement rules protect paths and structures while allowing vegetation beneath tree canopies.

Paths, fences and rivers

Stone paths gained spacing checks, modular dirt paths were refined around turns and junctions, and scattered fence clusters gained continuity rules. Riverbanks now follow the actual water shape, with sparse partly submerged props.

Board v0.1 locked

After comparing seeds and correcting repeated placement failures, I accepted the board's procedural logic for v0.1. Future work on the board will concentrate on the Romanian asset kit and visual presentation. Next: one playable third-person biome.

Devlog 02 / October 2026 · Environment rules

Once the roads worked, the next step was making buildings, trees and terrain respond to the generated network.

From scatter to composition

I separated the environment generator from route topology and introduced road-aware placement, terrain variety, tree clusters and structures. Artist-facing controls manage density, size and clearance without changing the gameplay connections.

Seed variation

Different seeds needed to feel distinct while keeping paths readable and important locations accessible. The screenshots capture early layout tests before the natural-dressing pass.

Devlog 01 / October 2026 · Board topology

The starting point was a seeded hex board with routes that split and reconnect, not several parallel lines.

Routes that actually connect

I directed the generator towards a bending start-to-finish route with meaningful branches and junctions. Explicit connections are stored as board data, independently of the visible road meshes.

Reliable regeneration

Each new seed changes the layout while preserving legal connectivity. These early greybox captures show the road logic before environment dressing was introduced.

Workflow / Authorship

Artist-directed, AI-assisted Blueprint prototyping. I define the art direction, procedural rules, constraints and artist controls; test seeds, identify failures and select the final solutions. AI helps implement and debug Blueprint logic. Gameplay programming is owned by Abi and Robert; audio by Vlad. Current environment assets include Kenney placeholders.
